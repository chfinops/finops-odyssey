# FinOps Odyssey - Game Changes Summary

## 🎮 **Core Game Flow Transformation**

### **Question System Redesign**
- **Before**: Modal popup questions that paused gameplay
- **After**: Questions display at top of screen, answers are 3 large asteroids (20 hits each)
- **Answer asteroids**: Real answer text, same neutral color, rotating body with fixed text

### **Game Progression**
- **Before**: Time-based phases (2 minutes total)
- **After**: Question-driven progression: Question → Wave → Question → Wave (5 cycles)
- **Wave duration**: 24 seconds each, max 12 asteroids per wave
- **Phase advancement**: Based on question progress, not correct answers

## 🚀 **Visual & Animation Enhancements**

### **Rocket Behavior**
- **Correct answers**: 4-second advancement animation through cleared path with speed lines
- **Wrong answers**: 3-second crash animation with system damage effects and debris
- **Movement**: Rocket moves past asteroids

### **Epic Ending Animations**
- **Victory**: 5-second moon landing with guiding beam, tractor beam effects, dust particles
- **Death**: 5-second explosion with multiple blast rings, debris, and screen effects

### **Moon Visibility**
- **Guaranteed appearance**: Moon shows in Wave 4 & 5 regardless of answer accuracy
- **Progressive approach**: Moon gets larger and closer in final waves

## 🎯 **Game Mechanics Improvements**

### **Budget Management**
- **Paused during questions**: Time and budget freeze during question sequences

### **Accuracy Tracking**
- **Real-time display**: Bullets fired vs hits landed percentage
- **HUD integration**: Replaces "Efficiency" with live accuracy meter
- **End game stats**: Shows final accuracy in victory/defeat screens

## 🎨 **Visual Polish**

### **Asteroid Animations**
- **Answer asteroids**: Rotating body with stable, readable text overlay
- **Realistic tumbling**: Irregular rotation speeds for authentic space debris feel
- **Enhanced details**: Surface craters and spikes that rotate with body

### **UI Improvements**
- **Better fonts**: Arial for answer text readability
- **Dynamic messaging**: Context-aware status updates and wave announcements

**Total gameplay**: ~2 minutes of action + question time + epic ending sequences
